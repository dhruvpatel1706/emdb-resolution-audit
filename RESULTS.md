## What it does
A CPU-only, public-API metadata study with two evidence layers: an indexed census for historical, method and fitted-model summaries, and a stratified sample for criterion provenance, particle-count and Nyquist analyses. It does NOT recompute any resolution from density. The criterion and Nyquist checks cover 1,600 sampled resolution-carrying entries, not the entire archive, and the mandate comparison is observational rather than causal.

## Data
- **Full census:** 46,900 entries from the EBI Search API (100 at a time), each with method, reported resolution, deposition date, fitted-PDB-model flag. 45,151 carry a resolution. -> `data/raw/emdb_census.csv`
- **Consistency sample:** 1,600 entries from the EMDB native entry API (800 deposited before, 800 on/after 2022-02-01), each with resolution criterion (`res_type` + FSC method token), particle count, pixel spacing. -> `data/raw/emdb_detail.csv`
- All public, no auth, no paid APIs. CPU-only, deterministic.

## Headline findings (every number verified against emdb.json)
- **Resolution revolution:** median reported resolution ~15-20A through 2014 -> 8.9A in 2015 (direct-detector inflection) -> 4.5A by 2018 -> 3.3A by 2024. Fraction at 4A-or-better: 1% (2012) -> 38% (2018) -> 76% (2024). Annual depositions grow from a few hundred to ~9,000. Overall median across 45,151 resolutions = 3.6A.
- **By method (full census):** single particle 39,624 (39,568 with res) @ 3.5A; helical 2,067 (2,062) @ 3.6A; electron (2D) crystallography 192 (141) @ 2.1A; subtomogram averaging 2,725 (2,628) @ 21.8A; tomography 2,292 (752) @ 25.0A. Medians are over the with-resolution subset, near-complete except tomography (only 32.8% carry a resolution). Matches the physics of each technique.
- **Fitted-model link:** fraction with a deposited PDB model falls monotonically with resolution: 79% (<3A) -> 77% (3-4A) -> 59% (4-6A) -> 34% (6-10A) -> 8% (>10A).
- **Substantiation (1,600-entry sample):** all 1,600 flag resolution "BY AUTHOR" (self-reported, not recomputed). Criterion: FSC 0.143 cut-off 85.2% (1363/1600), FSC 0.5 cut-off 8% (129/1600), OTHER 6% (95/1600), with tiny residual categories (FSC 0.33, diffraction/layerlines, nan ~0.6%). Census-weighted (re-weighting sample pre/post rates by the census split 21,683/25,217) = 86.0%; sample method mix 87.9% single-particle vs census 84.5%, so the figure is not a stratification artifact.
- **Mandate is "ratified not caused":** FSC-0.143 share by deposition era is 3% (<=2013) -> 47% (2014-2017) -> 91% (2018-2021) -> 96% (2022 on). It was already ~91% BEFORE the Feb-2022 half-map mandate, so the mandate codified existing practice rather than causing the shift. The naive pre/post contrast (75% -> 96%) would mistake a decade trend for a policy shock; the year buckets rule that out. Reported as an association read honestly against its time trend, NOT a causal claim.
- **Post-mandate exception description (new within the Patwardhan comparator, not an archive-wide finding):** of the 800 post-mandate sampled entries, 34 (4.3%, Wilson 95% CI [3.1,5.9]) do not cite FSC-0.143. By criterion: 24/34 cite FSC 0.5, 7 "OTHER", and 1 each FSC 3-sigma / FSC 0.33 / no criterion. Entry-level method slices are single particle 23/718 [2.1,4.8], helical 2/26 [2.1,24.1], subtomogram 4/51 [3.1,18.5], and tomography 5/5 [56.6,100]. These sparse slices may include study clusters and do not establish a modality or depositor rate. By year, 6.3% (2022, [3.7,10.5]), 4.3% (2023, [2.5,7.2]), and 3.0% (2024, [1.5,5.8]) have overlapping intervals, so the apparent decline is within sampling noise; 2025 is too small (n=23). Artifact: `data/results/emdb.json` -> `substantiation.residual_non_fsc0143_post_mandate`.
- **Nyquist sanity (reframed):** median ratio of reported resolution to 2x pixel spacing = 1.77 (typical map reports coarser than its sampling limit, as it must). 3/1600 (0.2%) report below Nyquist, BUT 2 of the 3 are NOT genuine over-claims: in both the reported resolution exactly equals the recorded pixel spacing (pixel size landed in the resolution field, forcing ratio=0.50) -- a transcription artifact in two low-res tomograms (EMD-45648 res=11.02/px=11.024; EMD-15154 res=10.0923/px=10.0923). That leaves ONE candidate sub-Nyquist entry (EMD-44581, single particle, 5.1A at 2.62A pixel, Nyquist 5.24A, ratio 0.973): only 2.7% inside its sampling limit, within plausible pixel/measurement uncertainty rather than a clear impossibility. Honest reading: zero to one genuine over-claim in 1,600; substantive rate 1/1600 = 0.06% if counted. 4.3% within 1.2x (aggressive but possible). Median pixel 1.07A. NOT framed as a rounding artifact (a true value at the 5.24A Nyquist would round to 5.2, not 5.1). Artifact: `nyquist.sub_nyquist_entries` in emdb.json.

## Honesty notes / scope
- **Metadata audit, not a map audit.** We do not recompute any resolution; we check internal consistency, not correctness against the underlying density. A systematically optimistic but internally consistent number would pass. Stated plainly.
- **Self-reported caveat is the one substantive limitation** and is foregrounded (every entry is "by author").
- **Sample vs census:** the time/method/fitted-model findings rest on the FULL census (46,900); only substantiation and Nyquist rest on the 1,600-entry sample. AGGREGATE sample proportions (85% overall FSC-0.143, 4.3% residual) carry ~1-2% sampling error, but the small per-modality/per-year residual slices have much wider Wilson CIs (n=5/26/51) and are now flagged indicative-only in the paper; only the overall and single-particle figures are well-powered. The year-by-year trend is far larger than its sampling error. Each finding is attributed to the right source.
- **Situated against EMDB's live statistics dashboard** (emdbstats citation): the by-year/by-modality distributions are already public, so checks (1)-(2) confirm the known trend and the contribution is the per-entry substantiation/residual and Nyquist layers the dashboard does not report. Dashboard contents not verifiable offline, but its existence is well-established.
- **No causal overclaim** on the mandate; the year-bucket confound was caught and the framing corrected to "ratified, not caused."
- **FSC 0.143 attributed to Rosenthal & Henderson 2003** only; the half-bit criterion (van Heel & Schatz 2005) is not bundled in and is not mentioned.
- Mandate dated "February 2022" to match the month-granularity code cutoff (`deposition >= 20220201`); the precise announced date is 25 Feb 2022, which does not change any finding.
